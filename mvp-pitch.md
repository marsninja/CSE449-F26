# MVP Pitch: Tips and Format

**When**: MVP Pitch Week (Week 10, November 2–6, 2026)
**Length**: ~8 minutes per team
**Format**: In-class team pitch and live demo, followed by questions

---

## Overview

MVP Pitch Week is your chance to show the class a launch-ready MVP before Launch Week. Come with a product your classmates can actually use, show it working, and use the time with the class to gather feedback you can act on before you go live.

Each team gets **~8 minutes**. Structure your pitch around the four parts below.

---

## Pitch Structure

**Why** *(~1 min)* Start with the problem. Why does this matter? Tell a relatable story or share a moment that made you think *"someone needs to fix this."* This is your hook — make the audience care before you say anything about your product.

**What** *(~1 min)* Introduce your solution clearly and simply. One sentence should be able to describe it. Avoid technical jargon. A good test: could you explain it to a friend who has no background in the topic?

**Demo** *(~4 min)* This is the heart of the pitch. Show it working — live, video, or clickable prototype. Walk through one core use case, not every feature. Focus on the *"aha moment"* where the product clicks for the audience.

**Questions** *(~2 min)* Open the floor to the class. Use this time to answer questions and gather feedback on your product before Launch Week. Have a link or QR code ready so classmates can try your MVP themselves.
